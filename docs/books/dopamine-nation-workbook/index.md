# The Official Dopamine Nation Workbook

*A Practical Guide to Finding Balance in the Age of Indulgence* by Anna Lembke.

The workbook turns the DOPAMINE framework from [Chapter 4 of *Dopamine Nation*](../dopamine-nation/chapters/04-dopamine-fasting.md) into a sequence of exercises. You start by describing one habit, what you hope to get from it, and what it costs. You then plan a pause, pay attention to what changes, and use what you learn to decide how you want to handle the habit afterward.

Each chapter page includes a summary of Lembke’s ideas, reflection prompts, and a worksheet you can copy into a notebook or document. The worksheets and prompts are original adaptations. **Mina** is a fictional reader whose late-night video browsing runs through all eight worked examples, so you can see how one answer becomes the starting point for the next exercise.

## Before the break

1. [Data](./chapters/01-data.md) — Measure the pattern and trace when it began.
2. [Objectives](./chapters/02-objectives.md) — Compare what you wanted with what actually happened.
3. [Problems](./chapters/03-problems.md) — Identify immediate and delayed costs.
4. [Abstinence and Asceticism](./chapters/04-abstinence-and-asceticism.md) — Plan a pause, map cues, and decide what to make room for.

## During the break

5. [Mindfulness](./chapters/05-mindfulness.md) — Observe an urge and the reasoning that comes with it.
6. [Insight and Radical Honesty](./chapters/06-insight-and-radical-honesty.md) — Examine what you’ve been minimizing and what you can repair.

## Near the end and afterward

7. [Next Steps](./chapters/07-next-steps.md) — Compare what you gained and missed, then set a plan.
8. [Experiment](./chapters/08-experiment.md) — Review what happened when you tried the plan and revise it.

Lembke suggests this order while allowing readers to work at their own pace. In the workbook, a dopamine fast means pausing a specific habit or substance. Lembke often uses about four weeks for people who can safely pause the target and directs readers at risk of dangerous withdrawal to professional care.
