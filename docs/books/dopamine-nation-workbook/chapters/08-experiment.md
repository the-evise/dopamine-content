# Chapter 8 — Experiment

## Summary

The final chapter treats the plan as something you learn from by trying it. A setback can show where a limit was easy to renegotiate, what situation you didn’t plan for, or which part of the activity you actually value. Lembke ends with a review of what helped and one or two small changes for the next attempt.

## Reflection prompts

- Where did your plan hold? What was different on the days it didn’t?
- Did you enjoy the activity? How did you feel when you stopped and the next morning?
- Which part of the plan should you keep? What is the smallest change that would address the problem you observed?
- When will you review it again, and what will you compare?

## Worked example

Mina follows her plan twice in four viewing windows. On both evenings when she goes past the cutoff, she starts with the home feed and spends several minutes browsing before picking anything. On the nights she chooses a video ahead of time, she enjoys watching it and stops when it ends. For the next two weeks, she starts at 9:15 p.m. and opens the chosen video directly. At the next review she’ll compare how often she stops on time and whether she enjoyed what she watched.
