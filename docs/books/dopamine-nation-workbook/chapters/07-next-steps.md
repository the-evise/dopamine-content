# Chapter 7 — Next Steps

## Summary

As the pause nears its end, Lembke asks you to review both its benefits and its costs. That review informs whether you want to continue abstaining or try moderation. If you try moderation, define what it looks like in practice and decide what you’ll do if the old pattern returns.

## Reflection prompts

- What changed in the first few days? What was different near the end?
- What did you miss that still matters to you? What were you glad to have less of?
- What would a workable boundary look like on a stressful day?
- What would tell you that your plan needs to change?

## Worked example

Near the end of the pause, Mina notices that falling asleep feels easier on most nights, though evenings can still feel lonely. She misses choosing a funny video to watch with her sister. She decides to try two 30-minute viewing windows a week, Friday and Saturday from 9:45 to 10:15 p.m., on the living-room TV. She’ll review after two weeks. If she goes past the cutoff twice, she’ll move the next trial earlier and choose what to watch before turning the TV on.
