# Chapter 6 — Insight and Radical Honesty

## Summary

Lembke asks readers to look at what becomes easier to see during a pause, including habits they’ve minimized and ways they’ve explained their behavior to others. The chapter connects honesty with trust, intimacy, and the response someone receives when they admit a mistake. A second exercise looks back at a resentment and considers what happened, how it affected you, and which parts of your own response you can change.

## Reflection prompts

- How have you described the habit to yourself or someone else? What detail did you leave out?
- What were you afraid would happen if you gave a more accurate account?
- What can you take responsibility for? What happened outside your control?
- Who can hear the story and help you think through a repair?

## Worked example

Mina tells her sister she was up late because of work. A late call did happen, and then she spent another 45 minutes browsing. She had left that part out because she’d cancelled their breakfast and felt embarrassed. She tells her sister what happened and apologizes for cancelling. In her own notes, she separates the late call, which she couldn’t avoid, from what she did afterward. Her sister offers to reschedule. Mina also realizes she wants their next breakfast to start at a time she can reliably make.
