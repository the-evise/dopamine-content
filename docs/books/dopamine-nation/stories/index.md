# Story Threads

Five stories from Anna Lembke’s *Dopamine Nation*, brought together across chapters.

| Person | Story |
| --- | --- |
| [Jacob](./jacob.md) | A private compulsion escalates, returns after a year away, and becomes something he can talk about with his wife and his recovery group. |
| [David](./david.md) | Social anxiety shapes his education and work. After inpatient care, he starts to enjoy conversations he once avoided. |
| [Muhammad](./muhammad.md) | Academic deadlines help him abstain. Later, a camera gives him a new way to enjoy a trail full of old cues. |
| [Maria](./maria.md) | Sobriety changes how she handles an everyday lie and how she sees the progress she has made. |
| [Anna Lembke](./anna-lembke.md) | Romance novels become a way to escape. Taking a break helps her see what the habit gives her and what it costs. |
