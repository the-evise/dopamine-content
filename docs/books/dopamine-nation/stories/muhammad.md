## A promise with a finish line

Muhammad comes to the United States to study math and engineering. The freedom to use drugs recreationally feels new to him. He limits cannabis to weekends at first. Within a year he is smoking daily, and he can see his grades and friendships suffering. He makes himself a deal. He will stay away from cannabis until he finishes his undergraduate degree, gets through a master’s program, and secures funding for a PhD.

He keeps the promise. When he reaches that goal, he goes back to using on weekends. During his doctorate, the weekend limit gives way to daily use. He sets new rules about what he can use while working and what he can save for special occasions. Then he fails his qualifying exam twice.

## The celebration that moved the goalposts

The department gives Muhammad one more chance. He decides to abstain until he passes, however long it takes. For a year, he throws himself into the work. He writes a report of more than a hundred pages and later remembers the year as one of his most positive and productive. He passes.

The next night, a friend brings cannabis to celebrate. Muhammad declines at first. His friend dismisses the idea that someone as smart as Muhammad could have an addiction. Muhammad agrees to smoke once and tells himself he’ll wait until graduation before doing it again. Soon the rule becomes no smoking on class days, then no smoking on difficult class days, then no smoking before nine in the morning. A deadline had helped him stay away. Once he started again, each exception opened the way to another.

## A beetle at Point Reyes

While trying to get his cannabis use under control again, Muhammad goes hiking at Point Reyes. He hopes a trail he used to love will give him some relief. Almost every bend brings back memories of being high there with friends. Walking it sober makes him feel the loss more sharply.

At a lookout, he raises his camera and points it at a plant. Through the lens, a bright red beetle comes into focus. He notices its antennae and the hairs on its legs, then changes position to see what a different angle reveals. For the rest of the hike, he stops to photograph beetles. A sharp image takes enough stillness and attention to keep him absorbed in the scene, and he notices his cravings ease. He discovers a reason to enjoy the trail that he couldn’t have planned before setting out.
